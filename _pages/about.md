OpenLoco is an open source reimplementation of *Chris Sawyer's Locomotion*, the spiritual successor to Transport Tycoon.
The OpenLoco project started in early 2018 by the same group of people behind [OpenRCT2](https://openrct2.io).
In [September 2025](https://openloco.io/news/2025/09/openloco-v25.09.html) the project reached a major milestone with
*Locomotion* being completely reimplemented in C++. This means that the game can now be compiled for different
architectures (e.g. 64-bit Intel or ARM) as well as different platforms (e.g. [Windows, Linux, macOS](https://openloco.io/download/)).

To retain the look and feel of the *Chris Sawyer's Locomotion*, OpenLoco uses the original game data for graphics and audio.
Until the [OpenGraphics](https://github.com/OpenLoco/OpenGraphics) project is completed, OpenLoco requires a copy of
*Locomotion* to run (which can be bought on e.g. [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/356430/) or
[GOG.com](https://www.gog.com/game/chris_sawyers_locomotion)).

Curious to see what's been changed compared to the original game? Please refer to our wiki for a list of
[changes from Locomotion](https://github.com/OpenLoco/OpenLoco/wiki/Changes-from-Locomotion).
